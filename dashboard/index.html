How this data was cleaned

Three Google Form exports with different questions, column names and answer formats were merged into one model. Names, emails and phone numbers were removed and replaced with a hashed donor ID.

Source: One Health Life Savers post-donation evaluation forms (n = 281 raw, 273 after de-duplication). Self-reported data; deferral reasons are as donors recalled them, not clinical records. One donation counted as one unit of whole blood.
